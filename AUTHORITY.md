# Hayes Verify Authority Boundary

Hayes Verify is an evidence collection and evaluation engine.

It may:
- consume frozen EMS evaluation contracts;
- collect repository/platform evidence;
- execute evaluations;
- produce provenance;
- produce evaluation results;
- produce EMS return envelopes.

It may not:
- modify EMS control applicability;
- accept results on behalf of EMS;
- promote evidence into the EMS authoritative evidence set;
- override EMS control definitions.

EMS remains authoritative for control definitions, applicability, result acceptance, and evidence promotion.
